    Benefits — which schemes may fit you?

    Answer the questions under My details and this page checks six government schemes against your answers. It can only say "looks like a match", "check" or "does not fit" — it cannot enrol you, and only the official office or website can confirm. Every amount below was checked against the official scheme page on 10 November 2026. See Sources at the bottom of this page.

    Sources — where each fact comes from

    • e-Shram — Ministry of Labour & Employment, eshram.gov.in. Launched 26 August 2021. Free registration; age 16 or above; unorganised worker; not an EPFO or ESIC member; not an income-tax payer. 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN). 31.89 crore registrations as of 18 August 2026 (Press Information Bureau, Government of India).
    • PM-SYM (Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan) — maandhan.in. Age 18–40; monthly income ₹15,000 or less; not EPFO/ESIC/NPS member; not an income-tax payer. Assured pension of ₹3,000 a month after age 60. Monthly contribution ₹55–₹200 by entry age, matched by the government. Over 54 lakh enrolled as of 29 July 2026 (Press Information Bureau).
    • Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY — pmjay.gov.in. Health cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year for hospital treatment, cashless at empanelled hospitals. Eligibility follows the SECC 2011 list; all citizens aged 70 and above are covered. Check your name at beneficiary.nha.gov.in or call 14555.
    • PMSBY (accident cover) and PMJJBY (life cover) — jansuraksha.gov.in. PMSBY: ₹20 a year, age 18–70, ₹2 lakh for accidental death or full disability, ₹1 lakh for partial disability. PMJJBY: ₹436 a year, entry age 18–50, ₹2 lakh life cover on death from any cause. Both need a bank or post office account; cover runs 1 June to 31 May.
    • PM Vishwakarma — pmvishwakarma.gov.in. For artisans in 18 listed traditional trades, age 18 or above, self-employed in the unorganised sector. Toolkit incentive of ₹15,000; collateral-free loans up to ₹1 lakh (18 months) and then up to ₹2 lakh (30 months) at 5% interest. Apply through a Common Service Centre (CSC).
    • Size of the problem — Periodic Labour Force Survey / Economic Survey 2022–23: more than 90% of India's workforce is informally employed. e-Shram registrations: 31.89 crore (August 2026). PM-SYM enrolment: about 54 lakh (July 2026).

    Scheme rules can change. Before you pay anyone or hand over documents, check the official page linked above or ask at a Common Service Centre. Registration on e-Shram is free — nobody should charge you for it.

    My details

    These answers drive the benefits check. They stay in this browser only — nothing is sent anywhere. Fill in what you know; leave the rest blank.

    Privacy: this app has no account, no server and no tracking. Your details are stored only in this browser's local storage on your device. Use "Delete everything" under Money to wipe them.

    Kaam Saathi — built for the Code for a Billion Hackathon 2026, Challenge 09: Livelihood for the Uneducated. Open source (MIT). It is a record-keeping and information tool, not a government service. It cannot register you for any scheme and it never asks for your Aadhaar number, bank details or passwords.
